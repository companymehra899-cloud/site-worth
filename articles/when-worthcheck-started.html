When WorthCheck started

10 September 2026 · History

WorthCheck started in September 2026. The first public build went live the same month as a no-login checker: enter a domain, get estimated visitors, ad revenue and website worth.

Why it was built

People already used tools like SiteWorthTraffic and SE Ranking’s traffic checker. Those products taught the category: type a URL, see rank, pageviews and money. WorthCheck was started to give that same first answer from public data — Tranco rank, WHOIS, DNS and server country — without an account.

Launch timeline

September 2026 — day one

Domain search, live Tranco rank, RDAP WHOIS, DNS, geo, traffic model, daily/monthly/yearly earning cards, and a popular-sites table.

September 2026 — same week

FAQ, How it works, Privacy, About us, and this article series were added so the product could explain itself: what it is, when it started, and how the numbers are made.

What has not changed

Lookups are still free. Estimates are still labelled as estimates. WorthCheck still does not claim to be Google Analytics or AdSense.
