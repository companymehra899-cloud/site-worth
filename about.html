About WorthCheck

WorthCheck is a free website traffic, earnings and worth estimator launched in September 2026. Type a domain. Get a public-data report.

What we built

Publishers, buyers and students kept asking the same thing: “This site kitna traffic leti hai, aur monthly kitna kama sakti hai?” Paid SEO suites answer that, but they need a login and a card. WorthCheck was built as a simple public checker — rank, visitors, ad revenue and a sale-value estimate on one page.

When it started

September 2026 — launch

First public version shipped: domain search, Tranco rank, WHOIS age, DNS, server country, daily/monthly/yearly estimates and a popular-sites table.

Same month — report pages

FAQ, How it works, Privacy, About and a small article series were added so people can read the method, not only the numbers.

What is on the product today

  • Live global rank from Tranco, plus a 30-day rank trend when history exists.
  • Estimated unique visitors, pageviews, ad revenue and site worth.
  • Site facts: IP, country, ISP, registrar, created date, HTTPS.
  • A low-to-high revenue range so the estimate is not a single fake-precise number.

Principles

  • No account required for a lookup.
  • Show the method next to the number.
  • Label estimates as estimates.
  • Do not pretend we have Google Analytics of someone else’s website.

Not affiliated

WorthCheck is independent. It is not SiteWorthTraffic, SE Ranking, Similarweb, Alexa or Google. Those names appear only when we explain the category or the limits of our model.
