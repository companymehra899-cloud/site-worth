Privacy policy

Last updated 10 September 2026. This page explains what WorthCheck collects when you check a website, and what we do not collect.

What this service is

WorthCheck estimates website traffic, ad revenue and sale value from public sources. You do not need an account. We do not ask for a name, email or password to run a lookup.

Information we process

  • Domain you type — used only to fetch rank, WHOIS, DNS, geo and a public homepage title.
  • Technical logs — the server may record time, path and a generic user agent so the app can stay online.
  • Short cache — a report can be stored in memory for about six hours so repeat checks do not hammer public APIs.

Information we do not collect

  • No user accounts, billing details or advertising IDs.
  • No access to Google Analytics, AdSense or Search Console of the site you look up.
  • We do not sell search history or build marketing lists from lookups.

Third-party sources

A lookup may call Tranco, RDAP/WHOIS, Cloudflare DNS, IP geolocation and the target website itself. Those services have their own terms. Favicons are loaded from Google’s public favicon service.

Cookies

WorthCheck does not set tracking cookies for ads. Your browser may keep its own history of pages you visit, which we do not control.

Estimates, not facts about you

Reports describe other websites, not you. Numbers are models. Do not treat them as legal valuations, tax figures or proof of income.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Questions: see About us.
