What WorthCheck is not

10 September 2026 · Limits

WorthCheck is a public estimator. It is useful as a first look. It is the wrong tool if you need audited traffic or tax-ready income.

Not Google Analytics

We do not see sessions, bounce rate or real users of the site you typed. Visitors are modelled from global rank.

Not AdSense

Monthly earnings assume display ads at a country CPM. Many sites make money from subscriptions, affiliates, lead-gen or products. Those streams are invisible here.

Not SE Ranking, Similarweb or Alexa

Those products sell panels, clickstream and keyword databases. WorthCheck does not resell that data and is not affiliated with them. SiteWorthTraffic is in the same category of public worth pages; we are not that site either.

Not a legal valuation

Do not use a WorthCheck number as the price in a share-purchase agreement, a loan file or a tax return. Get books, traffic logs and a human valuer for that.

What it is good for

Comparing two domains in the same niche, explaining rank-to-traffic to a student, or deciding whether a site is large enough to research further.
