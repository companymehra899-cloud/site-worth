How estimates are calculated

1. Live global rank

Tranco publishes a research ranking of the most visited websites. Rank 1 is roughly Google-scale traffic.

2. Traffic model

Daily unique visitors are modelled as 900M / rank, then converted to pageviews with a typical pages-per-visit curve.

3. Ad RPM / CPM

Revenue uses country CPM from the site's server location, adjusted for finance/tech vs wiki/news niches.

4. Site worth

Sale value is estimated at 6x yearly ad revenue, the same style of multiple public worth tools use.

These numbers are estimates for research and comparison. They are not Google Analytics, AdSense or SE Ranking paid data. Actual earnings depend on ads, subscriptions, SEO, geography and conversion.
