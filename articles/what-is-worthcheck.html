What is WorthCheck?

10 September 2026 · About the product

WorthCheck is a free public tool that estimates a website’s traffic, ad earnings and sale value from a domain name. You type wikipedia.org or paste a URL. The report returns live global rank, estimated visitors, pageviews, monthly ad revenue and a 6x yearly-revenue worth figure.

What you see on the home page

  • A search box for any domain.
  • Sample lookups: wikipedia.org, github.com, nytimes.com, indiatimes.com.
  • A popular-websites table built from live Tranco ranks.
  • A short method strip so the formula is visible before you search.

What a report contains

After a lookup, WorthCheck shows estimated worth, daily visitors, daily pageviews and daily ad revenue. Below that are daily, monthly and yearly cards, a 30-day rank trend when history exists, and site facts: country, IP, ISP, registrar, created date, HTTPS and whether the homepage responds.

Who it is for

Publishers comparing niches, buyers doing a first pass on a domain, students learning how public rank maps to traffic, and anyone who wants a number without signing up for a paid SEO suite.
